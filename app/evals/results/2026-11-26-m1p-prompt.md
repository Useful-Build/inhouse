# M1-P's prompt, 2026-11-26: the dry runs pass; the paid run follows on main

## Question

M1-P (Stage 1) is the first prompt PR of the build (#441). It raises the system prompt from v3 to v4 (#391's links
rule), turns on long jobs (the server's own carry-on texts, `server/prompt/job.ts`) and sends the two helpers' words
(researcher and analyst, `server/prompt/agents.ts`). Do the existing cases still pass on it, first at no cost, then on
the real model?

  product target to end a turn waiting on an open question. #441 now ships v4 (v3 plus the links rule, `question_ask`

  `node evals/run.ts --target fake --out evals/results/2026-11-26-m1p-dry-run-2 --topic "M1-P prompt v4 dry run"`.
  63 cases x 3 repeats on the fake target (scripted answers, no model).
- **Paid run** (Q2 = A: Sonnet 5.5 medium, $20 in all: the product suite at $15 and the morning-check set at $5): on the
  eval deployment right after #441 merges, because `eval-deploy` deploys only app main's head. Its results and this
  write-up's paid section come in a follow-up PR.
