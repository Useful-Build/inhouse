# P7: Run (`fleet/run`)

Status: draft, 2026-11-19, revised after the Sonnet and Opus review. Phase plan under the backend plan
(`docs/plans/2026-11-19-backend.md`, Architecture 9). Common rules are in its "Phase schedule (waves)" section.

## Goal

At the Run stage the agent handles the brand's agreed jobs by itself, inside its limits, with every step logged and
undoable where the tool allows, and Pause stops everything.

**Success measure (the real slice):** an allowed restock runs by itself and shows in Activity; a refund still waits.

## Scope

In:
- **The policy check** before the outbox claim, only when the server's stage is Run: the action's operation is on the
  brand's job list and marked eligible for Run in P6's operation table; it reserves its share of the daily action limit
  and the largest-restock limit atomically in D1; money and customer actions always wait.
- **Handled jobs:** each logged in Activity and the audit log with Undo where the tool allows (a compensating action
  through the outbox), and Redo (`redoHandled`) where the contract has it.
- **Pause at Run:** P3's Pause (runs) and P6's claim check (outbox) are proven together at Run; the Pause switch in the
  UI reaches both.
- **Run settings:** the job list and limits on the Inhouse team's pages, read-only for admins, with `ifRev` and audit
  lines.
- A Run-stage eval set: tool data that pushes the agent past its limits or off its job list.

Out: new connectors or operations (each joins the operation table when a brand needs it); Pause itself (built in P3 and
P6).

## Design notes

- **A job** is an operation key from the table plus its units field (for a restock, the inventory adjustment's
  `units`).
- **Who approves at Run.** When the policy check passes, a system principal records the approval with its own audit
  actor, and the same 10-second hold applies, so Undo works before the claim exactly as at Act.
- **Limits.** "Daily" is the brand's day in its time zone. Undo before the claim releases the reservation. A
  compensating action after a send is exempt from the limits and audited as such.
- **At Act**, the same action always waits, whatever the job list says.
- **Wave ownership.** W2 owns `src/agent/mock.ts` and the settings pages in this wave; W3 hands its changes to W2.
- The policy design is written by the orchestrator (Opus) into W1's brief.

## Browser scenarios (`e2e/ship/run.spec.ts`, desktop and phone, test build, write store, scripted model)

- S1: An allowed restock runs by itself and shows in Activity with Undo.
- S2: A refund drafted at Run still waits for approval.
- S3: The next restock past the daily limit waits instead of running.
- S4: Pause stops a live run and a pending send; Resume starts nothing by itself.
- S5: Undo on a handled restock inside the hold stops it; Undo after the send creates its compensating action, and
  Activity shows both.
- S6: As an admin, the Run settings are read-only.

## Acceptance

1. At Run, a job inside its limits runs by itself after the hold; one outside the list or past a limit waits; money
   and customer actions always wait.
2. At Act, with the same job list, the same action waits.
3. Limit reservations are atomic: parallel claims never exceed the daily or largest-restock limit (a concurrency test);
   Undo before the claim releases the reservation.
4. Pause stops new runs, live runs and the outbox at the claim; nothing sends while paused.
5. Every handled job is in Activity and the audit log with the system approval actor; Undo and Redo work where the
   tool allows and are absent where it doesn't.
6. The Run-stage eval set has zero limit or list bypasses; results are kept.
7. API specs and the contract suite pass for P7 methods; the demo is unchanged.
8. QA PASS and the ship recording on S1 to S6; the real-model smoke passes on the house deployment.

## Must not

No action outside the job list or limits; no money or customer action without a person; no auto-run below the Run
stage; no send while paused; no limit enforced only in the UI.

## Risks

- Limits under concurrency: one conditional update per reservation, and a property test over interleavings.
- Undo semantics differ per tool; the operation table states it, and the UI shows Undo only where it's real.
